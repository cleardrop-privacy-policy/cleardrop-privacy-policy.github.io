Cleardrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

In short: Cleardrop collects no personal data. It does not track you, has no analytics, and sends nothing to any server. Everything it does happens inside your browser.

What Cleardrop does

Cleardrop is a browser extension that restyles websites' navigation bars, toolbars, menus and buttons as glass, and adds an optional ambient glow around videos and GIFs. To do this it runs on the pages you visit and reads their layout and colors locally, in your browser.

Information we collect

None. Cleardrop does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information, browsing history, page content or usage data. The developer never receives any data from the extension.

How pages are processed

  • Cleardrop inspects the structure and colors of the page you are viewing to decide where to apply the glass effect and to keep text readable.
  • For the ambient glow, it samples a tiny, low-resolution set of edge colors from videos and GIFs playing on the page.
  • All of this happens on your device, in memory, and is discarded right away. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Settings stored in your browser

Cleardrop saves your preferences with the browser's built-in extension storage:

  • Your settings (for example on/off, strength, background, ambient light) and the site lists you choose (sites where Cleardrop is always on, never on, or set per site).
  • A list of sites where Cleardrop paused itself automatically to protect performance.

If you have browser sync turned on, your browser may sync these settings between your own devices through your browser account (for example your Google account). That sync is handled by your browser provider, not by Cleardrop. Removing the extension deletes this stored data.

Network requests

Cleardrop makes no network requests of its own and loads no remote code. The popup has two links, "Report a problem" (a feedback form hosted by Tally) and "Buy me a coffee" (Buy Me a Coffee). They only open when you click them, and anything you submit there is covered by those services' own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Access to websites: needed to apply the glass effect and ambient glow to the pages you visit. Used only locally, as described above.
  • Storage: to remember your settings.
  • Active tab: so the popup can show and change the setting for the site you are on.

Children's privacy

Cleardrop collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the Cleardrop feedback form.
